## 2. What went wrong: evidence and hypotheses

### 2.1 Pipeline selection defect (confirmed)

`pipeline13-15.sh` follows plan Task 13 Steps 5–6 literally.

- **Stop rule:** continue while `near_dodge_rate` improves.
- **Final pick:** maximum `near_dodge_rate`, with ties broken by beacons.
- **Missing checks:** neither step looks at ghost dodge rate, beacons or E1/E2, so both degenerate rounds counted as progress (0.27 → 0.93 → 1.0).

This is a plan defect: the plan's stop rule can be gamed by exactly this failure. Proposed guard, which the user has not yet approved:

- a round is eligible only if beacons/min ≥ ½ of round 0's, **and** ghost dodge ≤ 0.3, **and** E2 passes;
- rank eligible rounds by near_dodge, and fall back to round 0.

### 2.2 Entropy coefficient starts at 1.0 when the actor unfreezes (strong lead, not yet verified)

`build_sac` (`python/fly_drone/sac.py:276-309`) sets no `ent_coef` or `target_entropy`, so SB3 falls back to its defaults:

- automatic α with initial value **1.0**;
- target entropy −dim(A).

`WarmupSAC` freezes α at 1.0 for the 50k warm-up frames, and the round-0 decoder `.zip` from `init_decoder` also carries α = 1.0. Once the actor unfreezes at 50k, α starts at about 1 and decays slowly.

Series from the round logs, as frames: ent_coef, ep_rew_mean:

| round3-encoder (killed) | α about 1e-29; ep_rew about −550                                                                                        |

The free-roam reward (`python/fly_drone/env.py:425-469`) is small per step:

- +0.05 alive, −0.05‖a‖²
- +20 per beacon, +2 × visible progress, +0.5 per new cell
- −2·exp(−clearance²/0.2), an altitude penalty, −20 per collision

With α between 0.1 and 1 for roughly the first 30–60k frames after unfreezing, the entropy bonus (α × several nats per step, also accumulated in the soft Q target) is likely to swamp that reward. That would push the actor toward high-entropy, erratic output before α decays.

After the decay the policy is left with α ≈ 0 and nothing to anchor it to the cloned or DAgger skill (see also the other session's spec §2.5, "Decoder rounds have no anchor"). Round 2 inherited the broken round-1 pair: its encoder rewards sat at about −2500, and its decoder only recovered to about −1200.

**This is a hypothesis.** Check it with D1–D3 (§3) before changing code. Possible fixes, which need user approval because they change the approved training design (spec §4):

- a small fixed or initial α (e.g. `ent_coef="auto_0.01"`, or a fixed 0.005);
- a target entropy matched to the starting log_std of −2.5;
- keeping log_std bounded;
- a behaviour-cloning anchor (KL/MSE to the round-0 actor) during decoder rounds.

### 2.3 Other observations to account for

- **Round 1 decoder reward dropped during warm-up** (−272 at 9k to −677 at 36k) while its actor was still frozen at the round-0 decoder weights. The round-1 **encoder** may already have broken the pair before decoder SAC did anything. Round-1 encoder + round-0 decoder was never validated. D1 settles this.
- **The round-1 encoder's own reward looked fine** (−159 → −151, improving after 108k), but those rollouts were stochastic (log_std −2.5), while validation uses the deterministic mean.

- **The E1 bar is not met even by v4** (0.687) or round 0 (0.686). The other session's spec §2.1–2.2 argues E1/E2 measure the policy too and that the bar may be unattainable. That is the user's decision; don't relax anything.
