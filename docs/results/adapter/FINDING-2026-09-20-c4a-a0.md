# C4a A0 — the connectome carries a sided escape signal

**Status:** recorded 2026-09-20. Free probe (`scripts/escape_side_probe.py`); artifact

No code, threshold or bridge changed.

## Result

The declared bridge's `escape` readout is the **mean over both `DNp01` giant-fibre descending
neurons**, which discards the side. Kept separate (DNp01 feature indices `L=1`, `R=0`), they encode
the loom side cleanly on the declared battery:

| stimulus | `DNp01` L | `DNp01` R | L − R | std (8 seeds) |
| -------- | --------- | --------- | ----- | ------------- |
| rest | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |
| light_l / light_r | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |
| **loom_l** | 0.8647 | 0.6081 | **+0.2566** | 0.0174 |
| **loom_r** | 0.6887 | 0.8647 | **−0.1760** | 0.0296 |
| loom_both | 0.8647 | 0.8647 | 0.0000 | 0.0000 |

- The side signal is **opposite-signed** for the two lateralised looms and **zero** for a symmetric
  loom, and is **≥5× the rest noise floor** (which is exactly 0: rendering is deterministic and the
  rest state is silent). `side_signal_separated = true`.
- A lateral loom has a global component (both cells rise) plus a lateral one (the L−R term is the
  side); the mean currently used cancels the lateral term.

## Conclusion

**C4a is implementable from neurons** — the side comes from `DNp01` L−R, no new readout (C4c) is
needed. The only change required is for the bridge to stop averaging the two cells and use both the
latch (magnitude) and the difference (side). Next: A1, the declared codec change (new identity), and
A2, the offline falsifier, before any seeds.
