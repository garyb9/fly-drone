## What changed

`adapter.py` gains codec **v3** (`declared-v3:467faae753cef735`), an additive, versioned identity:

- reads the connectome's two `DNp01` giant-fibre escape cells **separately** (`_escape_sides`);
- `loom = latch(mean escape)` (unchanged), `side = sign(escape_L − escape_R)`;
- `vy = −ESCAPE_SIDE_GAIN · loom · side` (strafe *away* from the looming side),
  `vz = CLIMB_FRACTION · g_climb · loom` (a declared fraction of the v2 climb);
- the v2 steering and forward drive are kept;
- `ESCAPE_SIDE_GAIN = 0.8` and `CLIMB_FRACTION = 0.25` are declared constants, not fitted.

`adapter.json` / `adapter-v2.json` are byte-identical; the default stays **v2** until v3 clears its
gate. `--codec v3` / `--adapter v3` select it; the viewer cycle is unchanged (v1/v2).
