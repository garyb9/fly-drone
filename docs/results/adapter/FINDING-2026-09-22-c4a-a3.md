# C4a A3 gate — v3's lateral escape is mechanical, not capable (rejected)

**Status:** recorded 2026-09-22. A3 of
[`../../superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-c4-escape-and-loom-selectivity-design.md`](../../superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-c4-escape-and-loom-selectivity-design.md).
Two committed artifacts, same seeds as the v2 smoke so the comparison is paired:

- [`adapter-check-v3-smoke.json`](adapter-check-v3-smoke.json) — 15 seeds × 120 s × level 3, seeds 1000–1014.
- [`liveness-check-v3-smoke.json`](../liveness/liveness-check-v3-smoke.json) — 15 seeds × 120 s × level 3, seeds 2000–2014.

**No threshold changed.** `roam_eval.ACCEPTANCE` and `liveness` are scored, not relaxed.

## 1. Verdict

Codec v3 does what the free falsifier said it would — it puts the connectome's escape response on
the **lateral axis at full magnitude** — and it stays **causal** (a blind drone no longer inherits
the dodge). But it **fails the A3 skill gate** and it **regresses a pre-registered liveness
criterion (L6)**. By the C4 spec's own falsifier (“any addition regresses … liveness → rejected”),
**C4a alone is rejected as a capability/default change.** Do not spend the 50-seed gate on it.

genuinely visual. But the skill it is supposed to buy got **worse**.

## 3. Why it got worse (hypotheses, from the data)

1. **The climb was cut.** Peak `vz` fell from 1.0 to 0.25 (`CLIMB_FRACTION`). Threats here are aimed
   at the _launch_ position (leading velocity), so a climb is an effective evasion and the teacher
   uses it (peak `vz` 0.6–1.0). Removing it likely dominates the threat-hit rise:
   `collision_kinds` (none condition) show **threat hits 12 → 29**, while wall contacts almost halve
   (**178 → 90**).
2. **The loom cue is not selective, so the strafe is always on.** The v4 cue saturates on turn
   sweeps (`sensory-model.md` §2), so `vy = ±0.8` fires constantly, not when a threat is closing.
   The result is longer, harder strafing runs: `mean_move_bout_s` 9.14 → 12.80 s and
   `bouts_per_min` 6.6 → 4.8 (both paired 95% CIs exclude 0). That is exactly the L6 regression.
3. **The side asymmetry is pre-existing, not a v3 bug.** Under v2, a left threat (`side > 0`) was
   already dodged far worse (0.25) than a right one (0.76); v3 keeps the same ordering (0.15 vs
   0.42). v3 did not flip the sign; it lowered both. The paired dodge change (v3−v2) is
   −0.23 with 95% CI **[−0.52, +0.04]** — directional, not significant, but never positive.

## 5. Reading and next step

- **C4a's premise was right and its mechanical claim is verified**: the sided `DNp01` signal exists,
  the bridge can command it at full magnitude, and the behaviour is now visual. What failed is the
  **closed-loop use**: a non-selective loom times the escape wrongly, and cutting the climb removed
  the evasion the body actually had.
- This is the C4 spec's own ordering: **C4b (selective loom) is a prerequisite**, not a follow-up.
  Until the loom signal separates a closing threat from a turn sweep, the lateral gain cannot help.
- **Do not run the 50-seed gate for C4a.** Do not re-label v3's ghost pass as an A3 pass.
- Open design question for C4b / a v3 variant: whether to **keep the v2 climb** and _add_ the
  lateral escape rather than trade climb for strafe. This is a declared-constant change and needs
  user sign-off (it is a different codec identity again).
