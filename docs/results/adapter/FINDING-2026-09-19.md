**Status:** recorded 2026-09-19. P0 is landed: the declared adapter is the default free-roam bridge
and the teacher-free causal gate exists. The gate **failed**, and this is the honest result the spec
allowed for (P0 falsifier: "if the declared adapter yields no useful behaviour, that is a real
finding about connectome/model sufficiency").

- Artifact: `docs/results/adapter/adapter.json` (`declared-v1:6a7de373f09cd571`)
- Gate report: `docs/results/adapter/adapter-check.json`
- Command: `fly-drone adapter-check --episodes 50 --seconds 120 --level 3 --workers 6 --seed-base 1000`

## Interpretation

The frozen connectome plus a declared readout adapter **cannot fly the quadrotor** to the
pre-registered standard. Concretely:

1. **Foraging fails.** The intact bridge almost never reaches a beacon (0.03/min, coverage 3.2%).
   The laterality of the 2,022 descending/motor traces does not carry a light-direction signal strong
   enough for a fixed codec to steer by; most of the time it is near the arena's motion floor (62%
   slow). This is consistent with the recorded structural finding that the motion/laterality path is
   not reliable (`docs/results/encoder-v6/CONCLUSION-2026-09-18.md`).
2. **Light-searching was never demonstrated** (A5 approach 0.00, steer 0.64). The adapter's escape
   gate does give a real loom response (climb/turn-away), but the dodge rate still misses the bar and
   the loom-off condition collapses dodging to 0.003.
3. **The body is not the whole story.** The spec's fixes for this — ascending/proprioceptive feedback
   (C1, P1) and connectome-constrained dynamics (C2, P2) — remain the proposed route. P0's value was
   to remove the teacher and measure the connectome's own sufficiency; it is insufficient as-is.

This does **not** show the connectome is useless: the loom/escape pathway is measurably causal, and
the ablations behave. It shows the declared bridge as specified is not enough, and that no teacher
should be reintroduced to force a pass.
