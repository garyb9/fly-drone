# C4b B0 — no front-end clears the E1 selectivity bar (both candidates fail)

**Status:** recorded 2026-09-22. B0 (selectivity feasibility, free) of
[`../../superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-c4-escape-and-loom-selectivity-design.md`](../../superpowers/specs/2026-09-20-c4-escape-and-loom-selectivity-design.md)
§5. Follows the C4a A3 failure ([`FINDING-2026-09-22-c4a-a3.md`](FINDING-2026-09-22-c4a-a3.md)),
which made a **selective** loom front-end the prerequisite for any lateral use to help.
No threshold changed; canonical artifacts untouched.

## 1. Question

C4b's gate: a candidate front-end must clear **E1 ≥ 0.8** (ROC AUC of the loom group on
threat-positive frames) and E2 (the light/loom margins), else it is not adopted. Two candidates
were to be compared: the **v6 spatial (retinotopic) encoder** (the documented fix) and the
**P3 relay extended to LC4/LPLC2**.

## 2. Method

`encoder-checks --controller teacher` (the fixed probe: the geometry-driven teacher flies, so the
frame labels are encoder-independent), 10 seeds × 60 s, level 3, current code.

- v4 baseline: `--policy docs/results/actors/v4-closed-s04/actor.json` → `b0-v4-e1.json`.
- v6 clone: `--policy docs/results/actors/v6/round0/decoder.json --encoder
docs/results/actors/v6/clone/encoder.pt` → `b0-v6-e1.json`.

## 3. Result — both fail E1

| front-end         | E1 loom AUC | E1 motion | E1 union | E2   | verdict                          |
| ----------------- | ----------- | --------- | -------- | ---- | -------------------------------- |
| v4 (current)      | **0.738**   | —         | —        | pass | fail (bar 0.8)                   |
| v6 spatial clone  | **0.720**   | 0.657     | 0.710    | pass | fail (bar 0.8)                   |
| relay → LC4/LPLC2 | _not wired_ | —         | —        | —    | **structurally infeasible** (§4) |

The v6 clone **beats v4** (0.720 vs the historical 0.687) but does **not clear 0.8**. Its motion
channel (Tm4/T2) is _worse_ (0.657) and the union (0.710) does not rescue it. E2 passes for both
(the loom and light channels are separable), but E1 is the binding bar and neither reaches it.

This matches the frozen-encoder situation: v6 encoder learning was paused at the neutral round-0
clone (`HANDOFF-2026-09-18.md`), and E1/E2 were **reported, not gated** under the frozen pair
(round 0: E1 0.720). The best available v6 encoder therefore cannot clear C4b's E1 bar.

## 4. Why the relay route is structurally infeasible

- The relay (`python/fly_drone/relay.py`) computes **global translation** (yaw/pitch) by phase
  correlation and injects it into the direction-selective **T4a/b/c/d and T5a/b/c/d** subtypes.
  M1b measured that T4/T5 do **not** propagate to the loom circuit in the frozen graph (LC4/LPLC2
  ≤ 0.001; `retinotopic-sensing-v6-design.md` §2.2). So the relay as wired cannot move E1.
- Extending it "to LC4/LPLC2" is not a retarget: a looming object produces **radial expansion
  (divergence)**, which a global-translation model by construction discards. Producing a loom
  current from the relay would require a _new_ declared expansion/divergence computation — i.e.
  rebuilding the very v4 dark-area-growth cue it was meant to replace (E1 0.738).
- The cells that _do_ drive the loom circuit are **Tm4 (primary, sub-region selective)** and
  **T2 (wide-field)**, plus the direct LC4/LPLC2 route — the v6 channel set. There is no cheaper
  declared route to a selective loom.

## 5. Reading and the fork

**C4b as specified has no adoptable candidate.** The documented v6 fix is the best available and
still misses E1 by 0.08; the relay route cannot be made selective without new declared code that
would just re-derive the existing cue. Consequences:

- **A2 (wall avoidance) and A4 (loom causality) are not reachable by the current front-ends.**
  A head-on wall is a symmetric loom; without a selective loom the lateral escape cannot time
  itself, which is exactly what the C4a A3 failure showed.
- This is a **decision point for the user**, not a code step. Options:
  1. **Train the v6 encoder** to clear E1 (the paused Option; a long run — needs sign-off), then
     re-run B0 and, if it passes, C4b B1/B2.
  2. **Design a new declared selective loom front-end** (a spec; likely a local-flow/divergence
     computation injected into Tm4/T2/LC4/LPLC2). Unknown cost; must still clear E1.
  3. **Defer avoidance** and put the effort where the readout is not the bottleneck (A1 beacon
     seeking, or body fidelity), recording C4b as blocked-on-E1.
- **Do not** adopt a non-selective front-end and label its behaviour causal; the ghost/A4 tests
  would expose it.
