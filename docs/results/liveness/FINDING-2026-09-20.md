# P3 liveness bar + optic-flow relay — smoke (negative on liveness)

**Status:** recorded 2026-09-20. The teacher-free liveness bar is landed and the declared optic-flow
relay is wired and causal, but the relay **does not make the drone alive**: the bar fails exactly as
the canonical v4 bridge does, and on the intact condition the relay is neutral-to-slightly-worse.
Per the plan, keep v4 as the default and record the negative. **Non-pre-registered**: 15 seeds, not
the 50-seed protocol; the full gate was skipped because the effect is null-to-negative.

- Liveness bar: `python/fly_drone/liveness.py` (`LIVENESS`, `liveness`)
- Gate: `fly-drone liveness-check` (additive; `ACCEPTANCE` untouched)
- Relay: `python/fly_drone/relay.py` (`optic-flow-relay-v1`); adapter
  `docs/results/adapter/adapter-relay.json` (`declared-v1:e1dade5784ff48a8`, `visual="relay"`)
- Report: `docs/results/liveness/liveness-check-relay-smoke.json`
- Command: `fly-drone liveness-check --visual relay --episodes 15 --seconds 120 --level 3 \
  --workers 6 --seed-base 1000`
- Runtime: 1 840 s. Teacher never in the loop.

## What the relay does

A declared, deterministic front-end: per rendered frame, phase correlation (sub-pixel) of each eye
against the previous frame gives signed yaw/pitch flow, rectified onto the T4/T5 direction-selective
subtypes (`T4a/b`,`T5a/b` horizontal; `T4c/d`,`T5c/d` vertical) by side. Offline validation against
the plant's own camera egomotion: yaw flow r ≈ 0.97–0.99; pitch under pure vertical translation is
weak (r ≈ 0), expected for a forward-facing camera. Artifact:
`docs/results/liveness/relay-flow-validation.json`.

## Interpretation

The relay is **wired and causal** — silencing the sensory field (which includes the relay roles)
still moves `slow_fraction`, and the relay-specific roles are exercised — but it does not change the
outcome. Three consistent explanations, in order of plausibility:

1. **Sensory richness is not the bottleneck.** The v4 cues already drive the drone to its motion
   floor; adding direction-selective motion does not raise the descending/motor activity that the
   declared adapter reads. This matches the fly.ai prior-art finding that the network sits near
   threshold and vision does little (`external-prior-art.md:71`).
2. **Chicken-and-egg.** 68 % of frames are near-stationary, so the declared flow is near zero most
   of the time; the relay cannot bootstrap motion it does not already have.
3. **The adapter readout is the limit.** Forward drive comes from power-cell activity above rest;
   richer sensory input need not raise it.

None of these is fixed by adding more sensory channels. The next lever is **what the connectome can
control** (more/differently scaled readout channels, or excitability), not more input.

## Readout audit (why the relay is neutral)

`scripts/readout_audit.py` (`docs/results/liveness/readout-audit.json`) applies each condition for
80 settled ticks and measures the change in the 2,022 descending/VNC-motor traces relative to a
matched rest run of the same duration.

| condition | descending max | descending mean | cells changed | top readout delta |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| v4 `loom_l` | 0.95 | 0.0242 | 186 | `escape +0.82` |
| v4 `light_l` | 0.58 | 0.0022 | 34 | `thrust +0.05` |
| relay all roles 0.6 | 0.83 | 0.0169 | 186 | `escape +0.74` |
| relay `cruise_yaw` (flow→0.5) | 0.65 | 0.0056 | 72 | `steer_l +0.06`, `steer_r +0.05` |
| relay `cruise_pitch` (flow→0.01) | 0.00 | 0.0000 | 0 | — |
| relay `yaw_neg_l/r` (full) | 0.45–0.48 | 0.0036–0.0043 | 57–64 | — |

Three concrete facts:

1. **The relay is connected.** Realistic yaw flow changes 72 descending cells and both `steer_*`
   readouts, and driving all roles hard reaches the loom pathway's reach (`escape +0.74`). The
   problem is magnitude and symmetry, not wiring.
2. **Flight flow is symmetric.** Both eyes see the same yaw flow in a turn, so `steer_l` and
   `steer_r` move together and the adapter's laterality signal stays ≈ 0. Asymmetric flow only
   appears near obstacles/beacons; in a sparse arena there is little to steer by.
3. **Pitch is inert.** A realistic vertical-flow current (0.01) moves nothing; the network sits near
   threshold (`relay:rest`: 24/2022 traces above 0.05, `power` ≈ 0.32 below the unit threshold).
   Even the loom pathway's `escape` response needs a strong, transient cue.

So the bottleneck is **downstream**: the connectome is near threshold and the declared adapter reads
a `power`/laterality signal that weak, symmetric flow does not move. More sensory channels will not
fix it.

## Verdict

Keep the canonical v4 declared bridge. The liveness bar stands as the prerequisite; it is not
relaxed. No 50-seed gate for the relay.
