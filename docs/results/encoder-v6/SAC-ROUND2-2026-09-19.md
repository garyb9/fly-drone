The init (the `it2` clone) forages 1.6/min; the round drove it to 0.30. Visible dodge ≈ ghost dodge,
so the dodge is **blind flailing**, not a visual skill. The shaped reward slowed the in-run reward
decline (−550 at 90k vs sac1's −789 at 72k) but did not prevent the collapse.

## Diagnosis

- The actor is frozen for the first 50k frames (critic-only warm-up): the reward is stable there
  (−304 at 18k, −301 at 36k). Degradation starts right after the actor unfreezes (≈50k) — the actor
  update, not the critic, destroys the clone.
- The dense threat shaping is therefore not the missing piece: the policy still prefers a
  collision-avoiding flail over sparse beacon collection once it is free to move, and the reward
  anchor (weight 0.2, decayed) is too weak to hold it.
- Both v5 and both v6 decoder rounds now collapse the same way, with and without shaping, with a
  reward-penalty anchor.

## Options (need the user)

1. **In-loss BC/KL anchor.** Constrain the actor loss directly to the reference policy
   (`sac1`/`sac2` used a reward penalty only). This is the standard fix (TD3+BC-style) and is the
   strongest remaining lever on the collapse; run a shorter gated pilot to test it.
2. **Better forager / gentler RL.** Start from `it2` and cut the actor learning rate and/or extend
   the critic warm-up so the actor moves less per update.
3. **Ship levels 0–2.** `it2` passes the round gate (beacons 1.6, ghost 0.15, E2 pass): accept a
   free-roam v1 with foraging and obstacle avoidance, and defer thrown threats / A3.
4. **Stop and write up** the frozen-v6 decoder-only negative result.
