## 4. Open decisions

1. **E1 group definition.** E1 currently scores only the direct `loom` group (LC4/LPLC2). v6's
   primary M1b channel is Tm4/T2 (`motion`); whether E1 should score `motion`, the union
   `motion ∪ loom`, or stay on `loom` is a definitional choice for the user. The bar (0.8) does not
   move either way.
2. **Injection cadence.** The runtime injects ~479 patch roles every brain tick (200 Hz); collection
   is ~1.4× slower than v5. Injecting once per 40 ms camera frame (currents persist) is a safe
   optimisation to land between runs.

## 6. Task 11 first attempt: the encoder collapsed (2026-09-16, 21:27)

The neutral round-0 encoder stage A (100k frames, anchored to the round-0 decoder) collapsed. On
real held-out clone frames the 100k encoder (`learned-v6:5beb8815b59206c6`) had:

| group | clone mean (std) | round-1 @100k mean (std) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| light | 0.711 (0.842) | **1.996 (0.009)** — saturated constant 2.0 |
| loom | 0.199 (0.292) | 1.443 (0.177) — drifted, far less varied |
| motion | 1.000 (0.002) | 1.040 (0.080) |

The in-run reward went *worse* through warm-up (−250 → −520) and stayed ≈ −450, improving only as
the encoder saturated — the same "frozen decoder lets the encoder wire it" failure as v5, in a new
shape (max saturation, not centre). The v5 entropy fixes (α pinned, `log_std` clamp) do not prevent
it.

**The v5-calibrated mid-gate was also wrong for v6**: `probe/action_state_std` is 8e-5 for the
healthy clone (random-noise probe) but ~0 for the collapsed one, so it cannot separate a good v6
encoder from a bad one. Replaced with `scripts/encoder_liveness.py`: per-group frame-mean std over
real held-out frames (healthy clone 0.81 light / 0.24 loom; collapsed 0.007 / 0.051; floors 0.10 /
0.08).

**Fix (user decision A): encoder anchor + anti-saturation.** `SacRoamEnv` now takes an `anchor`
reference encoder and adds, to the encoder reward, a decaying MSE anchor to the anchor's currents on
the same frames (`ANCHOR_WEIGHT=0.05`, linear decay over `ANCHOR_DECAY_FRAMES=100_000` per env) plus
a hinge on currents near the bounds (`SATURATION_WEIGHT=0.05`, `[0.2, 1.8]`), logged as
`rollout/anchor_cost` / `rollout/saturation_cost`. `train_round` defaults the anchor to the `.pt`
`--init` (the clone for round 1), exposed as `sac-round --anchor`. The two-stage driver
(`stage-v6-round1.sh`) now gates stage A on the liveness check.

**A alone was not enough — the entropy term was dimensionally wrong (the real root cause).** The
second run (21:42–22:19) had the anchor active (`anchor_cost` 0.024) but collapsed again, this time
to the **centre**: light and loom means → ≈0.995 (constant), so the bound-hinge saw nothing. SB3's
entropy term is `ent_coef * sum_over_dims(log_prob)`, so its magnitude grows with the action
dimension: v5's encoder had 8 outputs, v6 has 816, so the same α is ~100× stronger and the entropy
pull overwhelms the ~0.05/step reward, driving the deterministic mean to the action-space centre.
Fix: `ent_coef_init(action_dim) = ENT_COEF_INIT * 8/dim` for `dim > 8` (so the v6 α starts at
≈9.8e-5, keeping the summed entropy term v5-sized); decoder (4) and v5 encoder (8) keep 0.01
exactly. `apply_entropy_regime` now applies the same scaling. A latent bug was also fixed: a
decoder round under a v6 encoder was inferred as `spatial`, which would have built the spatial
policy on the 4-dim velocity interface — `build_sac`/`SacRoamEnv` now use the spatial path only for
the encoder learner.

**The anchor is not enough — a dense gradient is needed (user decision A).** The stabiliser only
changes *where* the encoder flattens; the frozen-decoder reward does not preserve visual variation.
The round now also trains the v6 encoder with the predictive auxiliary objective
(`python/fly_drone/predictive.py`, spec §5): predict the next DN trace from the current DN trace and
the injected currents (`PREDICTIVE_WEIGHT=1.0`), plus a variance term on the currents
(`PREDICTIVE_VAR_WEIGHT=1.0`) so a near-constant output is directly penalised. `WarmupSAC` adds the
term to the actor loss (predictor parameters share the actor optimizer, so one backward gives the
encoder its predictive gradient), logs `train/predictive_loss`, and re-attaches the head after a
`.zip` load (`_attach_predictor`). The anchor is strengthened to `ANCHOR_WEIGHT=0.5` with a full-round
decay (`ANCHOR_DECAY_FRAMES=350_000`).
