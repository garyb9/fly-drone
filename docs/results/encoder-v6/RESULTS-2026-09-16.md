## 4. Open decisions

1. **E1 group definition.** E1 currently scores only the direct `loom` group (LC4/LPLC2). v6's
   primary M1b channel is Tm4/T2 (`motion`); whether E1 should score `motion`, the union
   `motion ∪ loom`, or stay on `loom` is a definitional choice for the user. The bar (0.8) does not
   move either way.
2. **Injection cadence.** The runtime injects ~479 patch roles every brain tick (200 Hz); collection
   is ~1.4× slower than v5. Injecting once per 40 ms camera frame (currents persist) is a safe
   optimisation to land between runs.
