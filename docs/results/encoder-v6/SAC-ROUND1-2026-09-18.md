`ep_rew_mean` fell from −433 (9k) to −789 (72k), then recovered only to −590 (144k) — worse than the
warm start. The anchor did not hold the clone. This reproduces the v5 decoder-round failure
(degenerate dodger, beacons 0) despite the new anchor: a decayed action-MSE **reward penalty** at
weight 0.2 is not a strong enough constraint once the reward gradient pushes toward dodging.

## Notes / possible fixes (for the user)

- **Stronger constraint:** raise the anchor weight and/or slow the decay; or regularise the actor
  loss directly (a KL/BC term to the reference policy, TD3+BC-style) rather than a reward penalty.
- **Reward:** the free-roam reward has no positive term for dodging and a sparse +20/beacon against
  a −20 collision. The v6 spec §5 already calls for **dense potential-based threat shaping** and a
  threat curriculum; the policy currently games the collision penalty by flailing.
- **Start point:** `it3t` is a weak forager (0.80/min) to begin with; `it2` foraged 1.6 but had no
  dodge. Neither is a strong anchor.
- **Fallback:** `it2` passes the round guard on the screen numbers (beacons 1.60 ≥ 1.0, ghost 0.15
  ≤ 0.3); E2 is the frozen encoder's pass. A free-roam v1 on levels 0–2 (forage + obstacle
  avoidance, no thrown threats) is available now; A3/threat dodging would be a separate milestone.
