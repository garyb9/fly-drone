**Status:** v6 is **not finished**. Three encoder-training routes have clean negative results; the best

## 1. Where v6 stands

| Route | Run | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neutral clone + round-0 decoder | `runs/v6/round0` | Foraging gate **pass** (2.00 beacons/min, v4 ref 1.933); E1 loom **0.720** (v4 0.732), E2 pass. **Valid but not selective.** |
| `seed_motion` re-clone | — | Gate **fail** (1.50 vs 1.547): driving the loom circuit from a global scalar hurts foraging. |
| Alternating encoder round | `runs/v6/round1` | **Reject**: beacons 0.00, ghost 0.875, E1 loom 0.526, E2 fail. Predictive + strong anchor did not hold. |
| Joint training, single α (M4) | `runs/v6/joint/round1` | **Reject**: beacons 0.00, ghost 1.00, E1 union 0.492 (chance), E2 fail. Auto-α drifted 9.8e-5 → 0.048. |
| Joint training, per-head α | `runs/v6/joint/round2` | **Collapse at 100k** (liveness 0.000/0.000), stopped ~160k; velocity α crashed to 3.6e-7. |

The liveness gate (`scripts/encoder_liveness.py`) never caught the failures: the representation stayed
non-constant while losing loom/motion selectivity. **Variance is necessary, not sufficient; E1 is the
gate that catches semantic collapse.**

## 2. What "v6 finished" would mean

A pair that (a) passes `roam_eval.round_eligible`, (b) beats round 0 on the E1 **union** with E2
passing, and (c) fails E3 (a bypass decoder reading the 816 currents is **not** better) — i.e. the
M4 acceptance from the v6 spec §6/§8. Note the pre-registered E1 bar (0.8) is above **both** v4
(0.732) and round 0 (0.720), so reaching it is a separate, harder question than beating round 0.

Alternatively, "finished" may mean an explicit decision to stop tuning the encoder and ship the
round-0 pair into decoder training.

## 3. The blocking decision (JOINT §6)

- **Option 2 — decouple the critic.** Keep the velocity head out of the joint entropy path; train it
  against an anchored encoder, and update the encoder only through the predictive objective plus an
  E1-style anti-wire objective. This is the remaining research attempt to make the encoder *improve*
  E1.
- **Option 3 — freeze the encoder.** Accept the neutral clone and train **only the decoder**. This
  abandons "SAC improves E1" but preserves the whole v6 sensing pipeline (patch currents, spatial
  silencing, retinotopic map) and unblocks free-roam behaviour now.

Tension to resolve: the project goal (AGENTS.md, v6 spec §2) deliberately chose a **learned** encoder
for free roam, so option 3 is a scoped exception that should be declared, not a silent revert. The
survey in [`docs/external-prior-art.md`](../../external-prior-art.md) is relevant: every strong
external project treats the visual front end as fixed/engineered and puts learning in the readout, so
option 3 is the well-trodden route; option 2 is the project's distinctive bet.
