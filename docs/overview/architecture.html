fly-drone · architecture blueprint · encoder v4 · 2026-09-14

The connectome flies. The drone is its body.

A frozen 166,700-neuron fly brain sees through the drone's two cameras, and the drone moves because of what its descending and motor neurons do. The drone adds only reflex-level stabilisation, the job a fly's own nerve cord and halteres do. It never decides where to go. This page is the map: what is wired, what is learned, what we have measured, and what comes next.

There is no hand-wired "this neuron is thrust" map to the drone. Flies have no rotors, so the decoder learns how descending and motor activity translates into the four things a quadrotor can do. The anatomical readouts (wing, thrust, steer, escape) drive only the illustrative fly.

One decision, 40 ms

brain_tick = physics_tick / 5 + 40

Time advances by ticks, not wall clock. Each neuron integrates v ← e−Δt/τm·v + Isyn + Iinj + b + σξ and spikes at v ≥ 1. A spike reaches the next neuron one tick later. The decoder reads a 200 ms activity trace, which is the brain's short-term memory within a frame.

How the decoder learns

imitation warm start → reinforcement learning (PPO)

Yes, this is RL, with a warm start. Reinforcement learning from scratch on 2,022 noisy traces is sample-hungry, so the decoder first imitates a sight-gated teacher with DAgger (behaviour cloning in closed loop). PPO then fine-tunes it on the free-roam reward. Both stages change only the amber decoder.

Evidence so far

Where the thrown ball is aimed decides whether dodging proves sight

teacher, sees threat teacher, threat invisible random flight

Scored as pre-registered: only throws that hit or came within 2 m. Aimed ahead, the sighted teacher dodges 0.90 (worst side 0.83) and the blind one 0.12, so a dodge now means the fly saw it. Confirmed on 20 fresh seeds: sighted 0.96 (worst side 0.92), blind 0.26, random 0.57.

Why the dodge climbs instead of sidestepping

Time to reach 80% of a commanded step (roam-step-response). Vertical is direct thrust. Lateral must tilt the airframe first, which takes longer than the threat takes to arrive.
