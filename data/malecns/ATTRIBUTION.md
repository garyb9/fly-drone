# MaleCNS derived assets

Source: https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY 4.0).
Credit: FlyEM / University of Cambridge / MRC LMB / Google Research — MaleCNS v1.0.

The binaries and JSON are generated by this repository's pipeline from public
MaleCNS v1.0 annotations, neurotransmitter predictions, synapse-count tables,
brain ROI meshes and identified SWC skeletons. Source URLs and SHA-256 hashes
appear in manifest.json, regions.json and skeletons.json.

Changes: edges below three synaptic contacts omitted; neuron indices reordered;
compact circuit and full-neuron graph emitted in the project binary format;
ROI vertices clustered at 4096 nm; coordinates uniformly recentered, scaled and
axis-flipped into the browser scene. Unknown/modulatory transmitters retain
anatomical contacts but provide no direct simulated current. Missing soma
locations are flagged and omitted from rendering, not fabricated. Neuronal
states, tonic currents and body forces are models, not measured physiology.

Plan 03 extensions include 24 measured SWC skeletons (each records its source URL
and SHA-256), annotation-derived Mi1/Tm3 and JO-E input lists, and ROI membership
from the public Neuprint_Neurons.feather roiInfo field. Region weights sum
measured pre/post synapse counts. Region glow is a weighted cell-activity display,
not local-compartment simulation. All use the same MaleCNS coordinate space.
